**Loads and stores** (206,420 executed):

| Form | Share |
|---|---:|
| long form, base + 16-bit offset | 39.6% |
| byte, short form | 34.3% |
| long form, absolute address | 15.4% |
| word, short form | 10.7% |

62,465 long-form accesses (30.3% of all loads and stores) used a base register with an offset that would fit in 8 signed bits, so they were long only because the short form's 4-bit unsigned offset couldn't reach. Nearly all of them are in `life` (neighbour cells 34-70 bytes away) and `matmul` (row strides); 1 had negative offsets and 0 were relative to `sp`. In this implementation the long forms cost no extra cycles, only code space.

**Calls**: 48,194 executed (2.1% of instructions). Most called: `umul16` 22,153 calls, 85 cycles per call; `fib` 8,361 calls, 30 cycles per call; `smul16` 7,173 calls, 24 cycles per call; `solve` 2,057 calls, 96 cycles per call; `putc` 1,695 calls, 6 cycles per call.
