The cut weights the 35 nets on the critical paths (`heron mini boards --tune`, `board-weights.json`).

## What could be left out

Each taken out of the netlist: the chips it removes, less the packages it adds; the gates that go with it aren't counted. Its time on the suite and on the C programs: *measured* on the register-transfer model's variant, or *estimated* from the profile (how often the microinstructions it slows run, and by how much: the rule is in `reduce.py`).

| Reduction | Chips | Suite | C | | What's lost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|

| no MQ: multiply and divide by shifts and adds of registers | 6 | +56.6% | +55.9% | estimated | a multiply or divide step takes three cycles more (about 15 ticks) |
