## What could be left out

Each taken out of the netlist: the chips it removes, less the packages it adds; the gates that go with it aren't counted. Its time on the suite and on the C programs: *measured* on the register-transfer model's variant, or *estimated* from the profile (how often the microinstructions it slows run, and by how much: the rule is in `reduce.py`).

| Reduction | Chips | Suite | C | | What's lost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| a prefetch queue of one word | 8 | +9.2% | +10.3% | measured | the second word: the queue refills more often |
| no reads at the A bus: the address from MAR or FPC only | 6 | +6.5% | +1.6% | estimated | a load at [R] (and [R]+) takes a cycle more, to load MAR (6 ticks) |
| one copy of the register file, read twice a cycle | 13 | +5.1% | +5.3% | estimated | the B operand from the register file comes a read later: a tick more where it's read (more where it's on the critical path) |
| no MQ: multiply and divide by shifts and adds of registers | 6 | +53.5% | +52.3% | estimated | a multiply or divide step takes three cycles more (about 15 ticks) |
| byte and half lanes in microcode: loads moved down, stores replicated, by rotations | 13 | +24.5% | +17.7% | estimated | a byte or half load or store takes three cycles more (rotating by the address's low bits) |
| no rotations by 8 and 16 in the result multiplexer | 8 | +17.9% | +24.6% | estimated | a rotation by 8 is 8 shifts, by 16 sixteen |
| the video in the one RAM: its address through the address multiplexer's fourth input, its words on the data bus | 10 | – | – |  | the video then can't read in an I/O access (the data bus is the I/O's): a long access, or several in a row, can starve it; two ORs on the address multiplexer's select |
