## Refills

A refill is a cycle that fetches the next instruction's first word and decodes from it: after every jump, and when the queue doesn't hold the whole next instruction. The suite's 568424 refills, by the instruction before them:

| `BEQ` | the jump | 20594 | 3.6% |
| `MOV m0` | the queue short of the instruction | 14847 | 2.6% |
| `TST` | the queue short of the instruction | 14235 | 2.5% |
| `MOV m7` | the queue short of the instruction | 13497 | 2.4% |
| `ADD mA` | the queue short of the instruction | 10238 | 1.8% |

**A branch-target adder** (not built): `BRA`, `Bcc`, `BSR` and `DBNZ` have their target worked out while they are decoded, so a taken branch's own cycle fetches at the target and decodes from it, as a refill does. Each taken one saves its first cycle (255033 of them); each one not taken gets a cycle as long as a refill's (6 ticks) unless the length class follows the condition. Net: 3.0% of the suite's time (3.4% saved, 0.4% lost).
