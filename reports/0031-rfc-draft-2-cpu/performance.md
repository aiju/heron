## Refills

A refill is a cycle that fetches the next instruction's first word and decodes from it: after every jump, and when the queue doesn't hold the whole next instruction. The suite's 586109 refills, by the instruction before them:

|---|---|---:|---:|
| `JMP mF s3z2` | the jump | 121528 | 20.7% |
| `BRA` | the jump | 76271 | 13.0% |

| `JMP m0` | the jump | 30658 | 5.2% |
| `DBNZ` | the jump | 25893 | 4.4% |
| `refill` | the queue short of the instruction | 20943 | 3.6% |
| `BEQ` | the jump | 20155 | 3.4% |
| `MOV m0` | the queue short of the instruction | 19167 | 3.3% |
| `MOV m7` | the queue short of the instruction | 13680 | 2.3% |
| `CALL` | the jump | 13138 | 2.2% |
| `LSL #1` | the queue short of the instruction | 11360 | 1.9% |

**A branch-target adder** (not built): `BRA`, `Bcc`, `BSR` and `DBNZ` have their target worked out while they are decoded, so a taken branch's own cycle fetches at the target and decodes from it, as a refill does. Each taken one saves its first cycle (256109 of them); each one not taken gets a cycle as long as a refill's (6 ticks) unless the length class follows the condition. Net: 2.9% of the suite's time (3.3% saved, 0.5% lost).
