# Fen: performance

Generated by `heron fen bench --write` (studies/fen/bench.py); what the
numbers say is in [report 0036](../0036-report-fen.md). The machine is
draft 2 at 6 MHz in the simulator's reference timing (D16-overlap: a
16-bit datapath), except for the column marked D32-separate, the
configuration of the built 1b hardware that report 0020's nucleus was
measured in. kbench (cmd/kbench.c) times itself with the board's
microsecond clock, so its numbers include the timer's tick, 100 a
second, and its own loop; the desktop's are the machine's busy cycles
(less its waiting in HALT), measured from outside.
