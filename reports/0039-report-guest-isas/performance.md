# Performance for report 0039

Generated by `heron v2_guests bench --write`; don't edit by hand. The study is [studies/v2_guests](../../studies/v2_guests/README.md).

Every guest's programs on draft 2's register-transfer model, with its own microcode in the control store and draft 2's length classes (4, 5, 6, 9 ticks of 20 ns). *Native*: the same programs in draft 2's own code, from [report 0031's performance.md](../0031-rfc-draft-2-cpu/performance.md): the C programs compiled by the Heron C compiler, and the hand-written suite. *Slower*: the guest's time over native draft 2's. A guest's reset routine, which copies its tables and constants into the register file, runs first and isn't counted.
