# Chibi Heron's 22V10s

Generated by `heron chibi hw gals --write` from the two-halves machine's netlist (`board2.py`); don't edit by hand. The design is [RFC 0047](../../reports/0047-rfc-chibi-heron.md).

Each output is a sum of products of the GAL's pins' nets: `·` and, `+` or, `/` not; `:=` a registered output (on the GAL's CLK), `=` a combinational one; `/NET` on the left an output whose macrocell inverts. `NET.OE` is an output's enable (it drives while that's true). The control store's outputs are `uFIELDn` (ucode2.py's fields; `_n` where the field is stored inverted). Terms are an output's product terms against its macrocell's (8 to 16); a 22V10 has 22 pins for its signals (CLK, I1-I11, IO0-IO9).
